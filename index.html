Atlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Atlas is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our travel planning application.

What Data We Collect

When you use Atlas, we collect the following types of information to provide you with personalized travel experiences:

  • Trip Data: Details about your planned trips, including destinations, dates, preferences, and itinerary items you create or import.
  • Email Scanning: With your permission, we scan your email to automatically detect and import travel reservations (flights, hotels, car rentals, etc.) to help populate your trips.
  • Preferences: Your travel style, interests, budget ranges, and other personalization settings you configure in the app.
  • Location Data: Approximate location to provide nearby recommendations and local travel suggestions (only when you opt-in).
  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials used for account creation and sign-in.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to enhance your Atlas experience and provide you with better travel planning tools:

  • Personalized Recommendations: Your preferences and trip data help us suggest activities, restaurants, and attractions tailored to your interests.
  • AI-Powered Suggestions: We analyze your travel patterns and preferences to generate intelligent trip suggestions and itinerary enhancements.
  • Trip Planning: We organize and present your trip information in a coherent, actionable format across all your devices.
  • Improvement: We use aggregated and anonymized data to improve our service, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • Communication: We may contact you about updates, new features, or important account information.

Third-Party Services

Atlas relies on trusted third-party providers to deliver our services. Your data is processed by:

  • Supabase: For secure data storage and real-time database functionality.
  • OpenAI: For AI-powered features that analyze your travel preferences and generate suggestions.
  • Google: For authentication (sign-in) and optional integration with Google Calendar for trip planning.

These providers have committed to using your information only as necessary to provide services to Atlas and are bound by their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement multiple layers of protection:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols.
  • Secure Storage: Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption at rest and access controls.
  • Email Scanning Privacy: Email scanning for reservations is performed locally on your device or through secure, encrypted channels.

While we employ strong security measures, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and maintain the security of your account.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: You can request that we delete your account and associated data at any time.
  • Export: You can export your trip data and preferences in a portable format.
  • Opt-Out: You can disable specific features like email scanning or location-based recommendations.
  • Data Correction: You can update or correct inaccurate personal information in your account.

Children's Privacy

Atlas is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will promptly delete such information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date and, if the changes are material, by notifying you through the app or email.
